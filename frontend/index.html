Frequently asked questions

What are your clinic hours?

The clinic is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Call ahead to confirm availability for your preferred day, especially for same-day visits.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the booking form above — it sends your details straight to the clinic's WhatsApp. You can also call +91 93841 54597 directly and book over the phone.

Do you see children for preventive care?

Yes. Children's preventive dental care, including fluoride treatment and dental sealants, is one of our regular services.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous dental records or X-rays if you have them, a list of current medications, and arrive a few minutes early to fill in a short intake form.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows, but booking ahead — online or by phone — guarantees your slot.
